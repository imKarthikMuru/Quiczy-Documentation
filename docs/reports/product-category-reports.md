# Product & Category Sales Velocity Reports

Understanding which menu items and merchandise categories generate the highest gross margin and sales velocity is crucial for inventory planning and menu engineering.

---

## 🍔 Product Performance Matrix

Navigate to **Reports** → **Product Sales**:

- **Top Selling Items (By Volume)**: Ranked by total quantity sold.
- **Top Revenue Generators (By Value)**: Ranked by total dollar revenue produced.
- **Gross Profit Margin per Item**: Calculates `(Sales Price - Cost Price) × Units Sold`.
- **Zero-Sales / Slow Movers**: Identifies dead stock items that have not sold within the selected date range.

---

## 📊 Category Contribution Analysis

Navigate to **Reports** → **Category Analytics**:

- Visual pie and bar charts showing sales share across categories (e.g., *Main Dishes 45%*, *Beverages 28%*, *Appetizers 17%*, *Desserts 10%*).
- Helps evaluate category sales mix against store targets.
