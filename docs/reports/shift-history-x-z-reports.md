# Shift History & Historical X/Z Reports

Quiczy POS stores permanent, tamper-resistant archives of every cashier shift, drawer balance, and daily Z-Report generated by the business.

---

## 📜 Reviewing Historical Shifts

Navigate to **Reports** → **Shift History**:

1. Select a date range or filter by specific cashier or terminal register.
2. The list presents all historical shifts with:
   - Shift # and Date
   - Opening & Closing Cashier
   - Total Collected Revenue
   - Opening Float, Counted Cash, and Over/Short Variance
   - Shift Status: `SEALED & CLOSED`

---

## 🖨️ Reprinting Historical Z-Reports

- Tapping on any closed shift opens the full audit breakdown.
- Tap **Reprint Z-Report** to output an exact archival thermal printout of that historical shift.
- Useful for tax inspections, accountant audits, or resolving cashier discrepancies.
