# Daily Sales & Business Analytics

Quiczy POS delivers comprehensive, real-time reporting allowing owners and store managers to track store performance, hourly traffic peaks, and revenue velocity.

---

## 📈 Daily Sales Dashboard Metrics

Navigate to **Reports** → **Daily Sales**:

| Metric | Calculation | Purpose |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Gross Sales** | Sum of all ordered items at sticker price | Total commercial volume before deductions. |
| **Discounts & Comps** | Total promotional reductions | Measures promotional impact and margin cost. |
| **Net Sales** | Gross Sales minus Discounts | Core revenue baseline. |
| **Taxes Collected** | CGST + SGST + VAT + Service Tax | Statutory tax liabilities. |
| **Total Revenue** | Net Sales plus Taxes | Total collected funds. |
| **Order Count** | Total completed invoices | Customer traffic volume. |
| **Average Order Value (AOV)** | Net Sales ÷ Order Count | Spending average per transaction. |

---

## 🕒 Hourly Sales & Peak Hour Analysis

The **Hourly Sales Breakdown** charts sales volume by the hour (e.g. *12:00 PM - 1:00 PM Lunch Rush* vs *7:00 PM - 9:00 PM Dinner Rush*):
- Identifies optimal staffing windows for kitchen and floor staff.
- Highlights slow business hours for happy hour promotions.

---

## 📤 Exporting Reports

- **PDF Summary Report**: Generates a formatted executive PDF report suitable for printing or email sharing.
- **CSV Data Export**: Exports raw transaction data for Excel, Google Sheets, or third-party accounting packages (Tally, QuickBooks, Zoho Books).
