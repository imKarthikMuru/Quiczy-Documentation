# Tax Rules, Surcharges & Packaging Fees

Configure statutory multi-rate tax brackets, GST/VAT splits, optional service charges, and takeaway container fees.

---

## 🏛️ Tax Configuration

Navigate to **Settings** → **Taxes & Fees**:

### 1. Creating a Tax Rule
- Tap **+ Add New Tax**.
- **Tax Name**: (e.g. `CGST`, `SGST`, `VAT 10%`, `State Sales Tax`).
- **Percentage Rate**: (e.g. `2.5%`, `5.0%`, `18.0%`).
- **Calculation Mode**:
  - `Inclusive`: Tax is embedded inside item sticker price.
  - `Exclusive`: Tax is computed and added on top of cart subtotal.
- **Applicability**: Apply globally across all products or restrict to specific product categories (e.g. liquor tax vs. food tax).

---

## 🥡 Service Charges & Takeaway Packaging Fees

- **Dine-In Service Charge**: Automated percentage surcharge (e.g. 5% or 10%) applied exclusively to Dine-In table orders.
- **Takeaway Container / Packaging Fee**: Flat or per-item packaging charge applied automatically on takeaway/delivery orders.
