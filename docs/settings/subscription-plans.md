# Subscription Plans, Tier Limits & Renewals

View active subscription plan details, terminal device allowances, expiration dates, and upgrade options.

## ⚠️ Subscription Expiration & Grace Period

- **License Expiry Alert**: An unobtrusive warning badge appears 14 days before license expiration.
- **Grace Period (7 Days)**: If a renewal is delayed, the POS continues to operate locally for 7 days in offline grace mode to ensure store operations are never halted during customer service hours.
- **Online Renewal**: Renew licenses through the web merchant portal or by scanning a renewal QR code.
