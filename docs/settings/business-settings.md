# Business Information & Store Profiles

Manage company legal records, multi-branch store directories, operating hours, currency symbols, and invoice numbering sequences.

---

## 🏢 Business Profile Fields

Navigate to **Settings** → **Business Info**:

- **Legal Business Name**: Official registered company name.
- **Brand / Trade Name**: Public-facing trading brand.
- **Tax Registration / GSTIN / VAT ID**: Printed on legal tax invoices.
- **Currency & Symbol**: (e.g., `$`, `₹`, `€`, `£`, `AED`).
- **Default Timezone & Locale**: Enforces accurate report timestamps and date formatting.
- **Operating Hours**: Define standard opening and closing times per weekday to enable automated Day Close reminders.

---

## 🏪 Managing Store Outlets

- View all linked physical stores, addresses, contact numbers, and assigned active registers.
- Only users with the <span className="badge-role">OWNER</span> or <span className="badge-role">ADMIN</span> role can modify business profile information.
