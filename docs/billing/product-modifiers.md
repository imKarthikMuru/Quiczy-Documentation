# Product Modifiers, Variants & Kitchen Notes

Quiczy POS accommodates diverse culinary customizations, item attributes, size variants, add-on toppings, and custom kitchen preparation instructions.

---

## 🧩 Modifier Types in Quiczy POS

### 1. Mandatory Single-Choice Modifiers (Variants)
- **Use Case**: Size, Portion, or Preparation style where the customer *must* choose exactly one option.
- **Examples**:
  - Size: `Regular`, `Medium (+$1.50)`, `Large (+$3.00)`
  - Crust: `Thin Crust`, `Pan Crust (+$1.00)`, `Cheese Burst (+$2.50)`
  - Meat Doneness: `Rare`, `Medium-Rare`, `Well Done`

### 2. Multi-Choice Optional Add-Ons
- **Use Case**: Extra ingredients, toppings, side dishes, or sauces with individual price surcharges.
- **Examples**:
  - Pizza Toppings: `Extra Mozzarella (+$2.00)`, `Jalapenos (+$0.75)`, `Mushrooms (+$1.00)`
  - Coffee: `Oat Milk (+$0.80)`, `Vanilla Syrup (+$0.50)`, `Extra Espresso Shot (+$1.25)`

### 3. Free Custom Kitchen Notes
- **Use Case**: Allergy alerts, dietary constraints, or bespoke prep instructions.
- **Examples**:
  - `No Onion / No Garlic (Allergy)`
  - `Less Spicy / Extra Crispy`
  - `Serve dressing on the side`

---

## 🛠️ Applying Modifiers in the Cart

1. In the Billing screen, tap a product that contains modifier groups.
2. A clean **Modifier Sheet** pops up over the product selection area.
3. Select the required radio options and check any desired add-ons.
4. If special notes are needed, tap **Add Custom Note** and type the instructions.
5. Tap **Apply to Cart**.
6. The item appears in the cart with the base price plus all modifier surcharges clearly itemized.

---

## 🖨️ How Modifiers Print on Receipts & KOTs

- **Customer Receipt**: Shows product name with indented add-ons and their respective price additions.
- **Kitchen KOT Ticket**: Prints modifier customizations in bold/highlighted text directly beneath the item name to ensure kitchen prep accuracy.
