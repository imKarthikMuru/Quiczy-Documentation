# Customer Directory & Profile Management

The **Customer Directory** in Quiczy POS centralizes customer contact details, order frequency, loyalty balances, and purchase history to drive repeat business and customer retention.

---

## 👥 Customer Profile Information

Each customer profile maintains:
- **Full Name**: Customer's preferred name.
- **Phone Number**: Primary search key and WhatsApp invoice recipient.
- **Email Address**: For electronic receipts and promotions.
- **Delivery / Billing Address**: For takeaway dispatch or corporate invoicing.
- **GST / Tax ID**: For business-to-business (B2B) tax invoices.
- **Total Spend & Visit Count**: Automatically calculated customer lifetime value.

---

## ➕ Adding a Customer During Checkout

1. In the Billing screen, tap the **Add Customer** button at the top of the cart.
2. Type the customer's mobile number.
3. If the customer is new, tap **+ Create Customer**, type their name, and tap **Save & Attach**.
4. The customer is immediately linked to the current bill.
