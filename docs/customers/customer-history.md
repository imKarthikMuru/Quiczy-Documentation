# Customer Purchase History & Preferences

Understanding customer dining habits and order frequency enables personalized hospitality and fast repeat ordering.

---

## 📜 Viewing Order History for a Customer

1. Open **Customers** from the main menu.
2. Search for the customer by name or phone number and tap their profile card.
3. The right-hand tab displays **Past Orders**:
   - Date, Time, Invoice Number, and Total Paid.
   - Favorite items and preferred modifiers (e.g. *always orders Iced Latte with Almond Milk*).
   - Past refunds or order cancellations.

---

## 🔁 One-Tap Re-Order

For frequent counter patrons who order identical items:
1. Open the customer's last order.
2. Tap **Duplicate Order to Cart**.
3. All products, modifier selections, and customizations load directly into the active cart for instant checkout.
