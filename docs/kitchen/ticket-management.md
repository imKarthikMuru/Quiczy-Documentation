# KDS Ticket & Item-Level Status Management

Quiczy POS provides cooks and station chefs with granular control over preparation workflows through item-level strikethroughs and one-touch ticket fulfillment.

---

## 🔘 Item-Level Status Progression

Kitchen staff can manage cooking queues at the individual item level:

1. **Pending (Unchecked)**: Item has just arrived from the dining room or counter.
2. **In-Progress / Cooking**: Tap an item to mark it actively being cooked. The item line turns blue.
3. **Item Ready (Checked)**: Tap again when the item is plated. The item receives a green checkmark with strikethrough text.

---

## 📦 Whole Ticket Actions

- **Mark Ticket Ready (`MARK ALL READY`)**: Plating the entire order marks all items ready and sends an audible chime / visual notification to the expo station or waiter tablet.
- **Bump Ticket**: Removes the finished ticket from the active prep screen and archives it to completed history.

---

## ⏪ Ticket Recall (Un-Bumping)

If a ticket was accidentally bumped or a chef needs to review past orders:
1. Tap the **Recall History** button in the top right.
2. The screen displays the last 50 completed/bumped tickets.
3. Tap **Recall** on any card to restore it directly back to the active kitchen queue.
