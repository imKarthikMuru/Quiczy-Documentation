# Expo Station & Food Runner Workflow

The **Expo (Expeditor) Station** operates at the central pass-through window between the kitchen and the dining room floor. The Expo screen consolidates food items arriving from different kitchen stations (e.g. burgers from the grill and cocktails from the bar) into a unified tray before dispatching food runners.

---

## 🎯 Expo Station Responsibilities

1. **Multi-Station Consolidation**: Ensures all items belonging to Table 4 arrive before runners deliver the meal.
2. **Quality Check**: Validates custom modifiers (e.g. *No Onion*, *Extra Crispy*) against the order ticket.
3. **Runner Dispatch**: Assigns prepared orders to available food runners or notifies waitstaff via tablet alerts.

---

## 🚀 Step-by-Step Expo Operation

1. The Expo screen groups orders by table and takeaway token.
2. When the grill marks burgers ready and the bar marks drinks ready, the Expo ticket flashes green: **"READY TO SERVE"**.
3. The expeditor inspects the tray, verifies the items, and taps **Dispatch to Table / Runner**.
4. The table on the [Dine-In Floor Plan](../tables/floor-plan.md) updates status to **Served**, and the ticket clears from the active pass window.
