# Welcome & First Launch

Upon installing and opening Quiczy POS for the first time, the application verifies the local license state and greets you with the **Welcome Screen**.

---

## 📸 Screen Overview

![Quiczy POS Welcome Screen](/screenshots/getting-started/welcome-screen.png)

_Figure 1: Quiczy POS Welcome Screen entry point on initial launch._

---

## 🎯 Available Options

The Welcome screen presents three distinct paths based on your business status:

### 1. New Business ("Create Account")
- **Use Case**: You are a new merchant or store owner setting up Quiczy POS for the very first time.
- **Action**: Tapping **Create Account** opens the [Business Registration](registration.md) workflow to provision your cloud tenant, company profile, and initial administrator credentials.

### 2. Existing User ("Activate Terminal")
- **Use Case**: You already have an active Quiczy POS subscription license issued by your organization, or you are setting up an additional terminal/tablet for an existing store.
- **Action**: Tapping **Activate Terminal** navigates to the [License Activation](license-activation.md) screen to scan a license QR code or enter a 29-character cryptographic key.

### 3. Direct Login ("Log In")
- **Use Case**: Your terminal has already been activated with a valid license key and you want to authenticate with your administrator/manager username and password.
- **Action**: Tapping the **Log In** link at the bottom takes you directly to the [Terminal Login](login.md) screen.

---

## 🔐 Permissions Required
- No authentication is required to view the Welcome Screen.

---

## 💡 Troubleshooting & Tips

> [!TIP]
> If your terminal was previously configured and you unexpectedly see this screen, check whether the device storage was cleared or if the license subscription has expired.
