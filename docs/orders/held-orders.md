# Held Orders & Parked Carts

In retail and counter environments, customers often step away from the checkout register to grab additional items, forget their wallet, or decide to add further goods. The **Held Orders** feature prevents counter bottlenecks by allowing cashiers to temporarily park carts.

---

## ⏸️ Parking an Active Cart

1. In the Billing screen with active items in the cart, tap the **Hold / Park** button at the bottom of the cart.
2. An optional note prompt appears (e.g. `Customer wearing blue jacket` or `Call back on phone`).
3. Tap **Confirm Hold**.
4. The cart clears instantly, allowing the cashier to ring up the next customer without delay.

---

## ▶️ Resuming a Held Order

1. From the navigation sidebar, tap **Held Orders** (or tap the yellow **Held (X)** badge on the top toolbar).
2. The screen displays all currently parked orders with item summaries, elapsed hold times, and attached customer notes.
3. Tap the desired held order card.
4. Tap **Resume Order**:
   - The entire cart, quantities, applied discounts, and customer links reload into the active billing workspace.
   - Proceed with scanning additional items or tap **Pay Now →** for settlement.

---

## 🗑️ Deleting or Clearing Held Orders

- If a customer abandons their order, tap the trash icon on the held order card.
- **Permission Required**: `canVoidTransactions` or `canManageDiscounts`.
