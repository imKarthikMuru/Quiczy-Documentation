# Table Operations — Move, Merge & Split Bills

Full-service dining frequently requires handling dynamic customer requests such as switching tables, joining tables for larger groups, or splitting bills among co-diners.

---

## 🔀 1. Move Table (Transfer Party)

When guests request a seat change (e.g. from an indoor booth to an outdoor patio table):

1. On the **Floor Plan**, tap and hold the occupied table or tap the **Move Table** action from the table details bar.
2. The floor plan highlights all **Available (Green)** tables.
3. Tap the destination table (e.g. `Table Patio-02`).
4. Tap **Confirm Transfer**.
5. **System Execution**:
   - All active cart items, fired KOT states, and notes migrate to the new table.
   - The origin table resets to **Available**.
   - Connected [Kitchen Display Screens (KDS)](../kitchen/kds-overview.md) update live ticket banners with the new table number.

---

## 🔗 2. Merge Tables (Group Parties)

When two separate parties join together or a large family occupies two adjacent tables:

1. Tap **Merge Table**.
2. Select the **Primary Table** (where all items will consolidate).
3. Select the **Secondary Table** to merge into the primary.
4. Tap **Confirm Merge**.
5. The secondary table items are appended to the primary table's bill, and the secondary table is marked vacant.

---

## ➗ 3. Split Table Bills

Quiczy POS offers two methods to divide table checks:

### Option A: Equal Split (Split by Cover)
- Divides the total final bill equally among 2, 3, 4, or $N$ guests.
- Each guest pays their proportional share via their preferred payment method.

### Option B: Itemized Split (Split by Line Item)
- Assigns specific appetizers, mains, and beverages to separate checks.
- Produces distinct itemized thermal receipts for each paying individual.
