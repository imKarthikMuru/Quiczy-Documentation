# Purchase Orders & Supplier Procurement

Quiczy POS simplifies vendor procurement by enabling managers to create, track, and receive **Purchase Orders (PO)** directly on the terminal.

---

## 📝 Creating a Purchase Order

1. Navigate to **Inventory** → **Purchase Orders** and tap **+ Create PO**.
2. **Vendor Information**: Select an existing supplier profile or type vendor details.
3. **Add Items**: Select products and specify ordered unit quantities and agreed purchase unit cost.
4. Tap **Submit PO** to generate the digital PO document.

---

## 📥 Receiving Shipments & Goods Inward

When the physical shipment arrives at the store:
1. Open the pending Purchase Order.
2. Tap **Receive Goods**.
3. Enter the actual delivered quantities (partial shipments supported).
4. Tap **Confirm Receipt**:
   - The app automatically updates on-hand stock quantities.
   - Updates the weighted-average cost price of the products.
   - Archives the completed purchase order.
